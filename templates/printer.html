How this works

Confirmed WhatsApp orders are printed by a small script (print_agent/agent.py) that runs on a PC at the store, on the same network (or Bluetooth range) as the printer. That script fetches these settings automatically - once saved here, it needs the printer plugged in / paired and left running, no further configuration needed on its end unless you want to override these settings locally with command-line flags.

See the README's "Thermal printer integration" section for one-time setup steps (finding the printer's IP, or pairing it over Bluetooth/USB and finding its COM port).
