How this works

Customers are asked once, naturally in conversation, whether they're OK receiving occasional offers on WhatsApp - only customers who reply YES are ever eligible here. A scheduled job checks daily for opted-in customers with no confirmed order in the configured number of days, and sends them the selected template, skipping anyone already messaged within the cooldown window.

Keep "Dry run" on until you've confirmed the history below shows the customers you expect - dry runs cost nothing and send nothing, but still show exactly who would be messaged.

The template must be MARKETING category and already approved by Meta - a UTILITY template isn't permitted for promotional content.
