How this works

You can connect a brand-new WhatsApp Cloud API number, or choose Coexistence during setup to link your existing WhatsApp Business app number instead - customers won't notice any difference, and the restaurant can keep replying manually from the WhatsApp Business app on a phone while this AI agent also handles orders on the same number. Chats stay in sync both ways, and up to 6 months of existing chat history is imported automatically.

Requires Meta Tech Provider approval for this app - once approved, set META_APP_ID, META_APP_SECRET, and META_CONFIG_ID in .env to enable the button above.
